Classification Guide

Recyclable Categories & Multipliers

Understanding accepted material grades, point rewards, and the environmental impact of your recycling.

🥤

Plastic

15 Points / kg

Thermoplastics can be mechanically shredded, melted, and pelletized into recycled flakes for fabrics, packaging, and construction pipes.

Accepted Types: PET (#1), HDPE (#2), PP (#5), LDPE (#4)
Preparation: Rinse liquids, separate colored caps, crush flat
CO2 Offset: 1.8 kg CO2 saved per kg
📦

Paper & Cardboard

10 Points / kg

Cellulose fibers can be re-pulped up to 5-7 times into new packaging, newspaper, stationery, and honeycomb cushioning materials.

Accepted Types: Corrugated boxes, office paper, kraft bags, magazines
Preparation: Keep completely dry, flatten boxes, bundle with twine
Forest Impact: Saves 1 tree per ~50 kg recycled
🔩

Metal & Scrap

35 Points / kg

Metals can be infinitely recycled without structural degradation. Recycling aluminum saves up to 95% of the energy needed for primary smelting.

Accepted Types: Aluminum beverage cans, copper wires, brass, steel tins
Preparation: Crush cans, remove non-metal insulation or handles
CO2 Offset: 4.2 kg CO2 saved per kg
💻

Electronics & E-Waste

60 Points / kg

Essential for recovering precious gold, silver, copper, and rare-earth magnets, while preventing toxic lead and mercury from polluting groundwater.

Accepted Types: Laptops, smartphones, PC boards, chargers, batteries
Preparation: Perform factory reset, tape exposed lithium terminals
CO2 Offset: 5.5 kg CO2 saved per kg
